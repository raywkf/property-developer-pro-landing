📌 What Is Property Developer Pro™?

Property Developer Pro™ is an educational and informational tool that helps you understand how a property development feasibility study is built — from the land, through the product and programme, to the costs, sales, returns, and cash flow.

It is designed for learning and exploration. Enter your own assumptions and watch a development’s numbers come together, section by section, with a plain-language educational takeaway on every topic.

🧭 What You Can Explore

The app is structured into 14 guided sections:

  • 1. Land Information — tenure, planning parameters
  • 2. Land Cost & Acquisition Considerations
  • 3. Product Mix & Gross Development Value (GDV)
  • 4. Progress Billing — Schedule G & H stages
  • 5. Master Programme — Gantt chart with critical path
  • 6. Sales Assumptions & Take-up Rates
  • 7. Recurring-Income / Operating Model (Per-Pax)
  • 8. Construction & Development Costs
  • 9. Groundworks & Earthworks
  • 10. Consultant Fees & Project Team
  • 11. Feasibility Statement
  • 12. Cost Apportionment
  • 13. Returns Analysis — ROI, IRR & Margin
  • 14. Master Cash Flow — Monthly with Peak Funding
